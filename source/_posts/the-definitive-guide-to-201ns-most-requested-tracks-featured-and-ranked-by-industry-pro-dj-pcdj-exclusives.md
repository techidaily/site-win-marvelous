## The Definitive Guide to 201N’s Most Requested Tracks, Featured & Ranked by Industry Pro DJ | PCDJ Exclusives

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/mostrequestedsongs-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/mostrequestedsongs-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "mostrequestedsongs-coverimage")

2014 has come and gone, and the results for the most requested songs (_and more_) of the year have been compiled by [DJ Intelligence](http://www.djintelligence.com/charts/).

Mobile DJ’s take note, as these charts are for you. There are hundreds of “_Top Club Tracks of 2014_” charts online, but it’s tough to know what Mobile DJ’s receive requests for the most throughout the year. Thankfully, DJ Intelligence has done the leg work and compiled a lists of the **most popular songs of 2014** including the Top 200 Most Requested, Top 200 Pop Songs, and Top 100 Most Requested of each decade, plus 13 wedding specialty dance charts.

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/dex3nowvailable-coverimage.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "dex3nowvailable-coverimage")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

Here’s a look at the **top 10 most requested songs of 2014.** What’s interesting is while their are some newer tracks on the list (_Such as “Happy” and “Timber”_), most of songs at the top are long-time mainstays. Without a doubt Mobile DJs should have these tracks at the top of their playlists in their [DJ mixing software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), no matter what year it is:

1. _**Journey – Don’t Stop Believin’**_
2. _**Cupid – Cupid Shuffle**_
3. _**Williams, Pharrell – Happy**_
4. _**Black Eyed Peas – I Gotta Feeling**_
5. _**V.I.C. – Wobble**_
6. _**Bon Jovi Livin’ – On A Prayer**_
7. _**AC/DC – You Shook Me All Night Long**_
8. _**Diamond, Neil – Sweet Caroline (Good Times Never Seemed So Good)**_
9. _**Pitbull Feat. – Ke$Ha Timber**_
10. _**Morrison, Van Brown – Eyed Girl**_

Here’s a link to view the [top 200 most requested songs of 2014](http://www.djintelligence.com/charts/DJIntelligenceMostRequested2014.pdf) as provided by DJ Intelligence. You can [click here to View All Mobile Music Charts](http://www.djintelligence.com/charts/).
